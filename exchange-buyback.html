Lifetime exchange

  • Available on any Elyra piece, for as long as you own it, against a new Elyra purchase.
  • Your new piece should be of equal or higher value; you pay only the difference.
  • You need the original invoice and, for certified diamonds, the original certificate.

Buyback

  • Available on Elyra pieces bought from us, with the original invoice.
  • Payment is made by bank transfer to the original buyer after inspection, within .
  • We may need your PAN and KYC details for higher-value buybacks, as required by law.

Conditions

  • Pieces are inspected by our quality team. Damage, missing stones or alterations by others may reduce the value.
  • Exchange and buyback values are based on your invoice, not on current retail prices.
  • Pieces bought from other jewellers are not covered.
