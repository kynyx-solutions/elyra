The bridal process

How we work with you.

Start at least 6–8 weeks before your first function for made-to-order pieces. Ready-to-ship pieces can be delivered sooner.

Consultation

A 30–45 minute call or personal visit to discuss outfits, functions, budget and dates.

Shortlist & quote

Photos, videos and a written quote for each piece: diamonds, metal, weight and final price.

Confirm & craft

Approve the design and pay the 50% advance. Made-to-order pieces take 15–21 days.

Fit & deliver

Quality check, sizing, insured delivery or studio collection, well before your wedding.

Planning tips

Getting your bridal jewellery right.

  • Bring your outfit colours. Share photos of lehengas and sarees so stones and metal tones match.
  • Plan for every function, not just the wedding: haldi, sangeet, reception and the days after.
  • Think beyond the day. Pieces that can be split or worn separately give you more value.
  • Check ring size close to the date. Sizes change with season and time of day; resizing is free within 30 days.
  • Keep certificates and invoices together. They support exchange, insurance and resizing later.
